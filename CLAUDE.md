**SECOND BUG FOUND AND FIXED (Jul 2026, deployment-readiness pass): sub-contractor pricing had the markup direction backwards.** `add_cost_groups_v2()`'s sub branch was dividing `sub_scope_price` by `SUB_MARKUP` (1.45) to compute "cost" — but the prompt (STEP 3B) explicitly tells Claude that `sub_scope_price` IS already OCC's real cost from the sub, before markup.
