# OCC Floor Plan & Elevation Drawing Rules

Rules for drawing basic 2D floor plans and wall elevations from site-visit dictation (CompanyCam walkthrough videos) plus photos. Confirmed with Jason Oct 18, 2026.

**Guiding principle: nothing is typical - it varies job to job.** Most plans will be interior rooms, but decks and sunrooms come up too. Defaults below only fill gaps; anything dictated or clearly visible in photos always wins. When the wall type or construction can't be told from dictation or photos, draw the default and mark it "(assumed)".

## 1. Sources and how they're used
- **Dictation sets the numbers.** Measurements come from what is spoken in the walkthrough videos (transcripts).
- **Photos set the layout.** Photos and video frames determine arrangement: which wall a door or window is on, door swing direction, sink side, what is adjacent to what, fixture placement - and wall/construction type when it isn't said (brick vs. siding, exterior vs. interior, deck framing).
- **Photos fill in heights that weren't said** (see section 11) - always labeled as estimates.
- **Match by time.** Photos/frames captured around the moment a measurement is spoken are tied to that measurement.
- **Proportion check.** Compare dictated sizes against what the photos show (e.g., a 57" vanity should not fill a wall dictated as 5'). Flag mismatches.
- **Conflicts get flagged, never guessed.** When dictation and photos disagree, mark the spot on the drawing and list it for confirmation.

## 2. Dictation format (per room or area)
1. Room/area name ("primary bath," "back deck," "sunroom") and ceiling height (interior rooms).
2. Walk the walls or edges clockwise from the entry door (or from the house wall for decks): "Wall 1, door wall, 10 foot 2. Wall 2, 8 foot 6..."
3. Openings: size + distance from the left corner to the edge of the opening. "Door, 32 inches, starts 14 inches from the left corner."
4. Fixtures/features: size + location. "Vanity on wall 2, 57 by 22, tight to the corner." "Stairs off the far edge, 4 foot wide, centered."
5. Wall type when it isn't a plain interior wall ("plumbing wall," "exterior," "brick," "knee wall").
6. **Heights that drive the price** - not every height, just the ones that matter for the scope: "Window sill at 36, head at 82." "Tile to 7 foot." "Vanity 34 high." "Niche at 48, 12 by 24." Anything not said gets estimated from photos.

**Capture habits:**
- Before narrating a room or area, take a wide photo from the doorway (or house wall) and one from the opposite corner so every wall/edge is covered.
- For each wall that has something on it, take one photo **straight on, square to the wall**, showing floor to ceiling. This is what elevation heights get estimated from.

## 3. Measuring convention
- Interior rooms: dictated dimensions are **finished surface to finished surface** (drywall to drywall, inside the room) - confirmed as OCC's normal practice. Wall thickness is drawn outside those lines. If a measurement is to framing (e.g., during demo), it will be said; draw it as framing and note it.
- Decks: dimensions are **outside edge to outside edge** of the deck surface (rim/fascia), measured from the house wall out.
- Openings are measured from the corner to the edge of the opening (not casing).
- Heights are measured **from finished floor** (AFF) unless said otherwise.
- **Closure check:** opposite walls/edges should match. Any difference over 1" is flagged instead of drawn.

## 4. Wall thicknesses (interior is the default)
| Dictated as / seen in photos | Drawn thickness |
|---|---|
| Interior wall (default when not stated) | 4-1/2" (2x4 + 1/2" drywall both sides) |
| "Plumbing wall" / "2x6 wall" | 6-1/2" |
| Exterior wall - only when said or clearly visible | 6" (2x4 + sheathing + siding) |
| "2x6 exterior" | 8" |
| "Brick" / "brick veneer" | 10" |
| "Half wall" / "knee wall" | 4-1/2", labeled with height |

## 5. Doors and windows
- Doors drawn with swing arc. Defaults: 2'8" interior, 3'0" exterior unless a size is given.
- Pocket doors, bifolds, sliders and French doors drawn by type when named or seen.
- Windows drawn as thin lines in the wall with width labeled.

## 6. Default fixture sizes (only when no size is dictated)
- Vanity: 21" deep
- Kitchen base cabinets: 24" deep; uppers: 12" deep, drawn dashed
- Toilet: 12" rough-in, centered at least 15" from side wall
- Standard tub: 60" x 30"
- Showers, islands, custom items: drawn exactly as dictated

## 7. Decks
- Draw the deck surface outline with the **house wall shown as a heavy line** along the ledger side; label it "House."
- Show stairs (width, direction of travel, number of risers if said), railings as a thin line along the edges, and any door from the house onto the deck.
- Posts, beams and joist direction only when dictated or clearly visible - otherwise leave off rather than guess.
- Note the deck height above grade if said.

## 8. Sunrooms / porches
- Draw like an interior room, but label which walls are window walls, knee walls (with height), or open/screened.
- Show the shared house wall as a heavy line labeled "House."

## 9. Existing vs. proposed
- Existing walls/items: solid gray
- Walls/items to remove: dashed red
- New walls/items: solid blue
- Remodel plans can be drawn as an existing plan and a proposed plan. Same colors apply on elevations.

## 10. Elevations
**Purpose:** show what's on each wall vertically - the info a floor plan can't carry (tile heights, window sills, vanity and mirror heights, niches, cabinet uppers).

**Which walls:** only walls with something on them - openings, fixtures, cabinets, tile or finish changes, or work in scope. Plain walls with nothing happening are skipped unless asked for.

**Keyed to the plan:** each elevation is titled to match the plan's wall number - "Primary Bath - Wall 2 Elevation." Wall numbers are marked on the floor plan.

**View:** drawn as if standing in the room facing the wall. Left on the elevation = the left corner used in dictation, so "starts 14 inches from the left corner" lands the same place on both drawings.

**What's drawn:**
- Wall outline: overall width (from the plan) by ceiling height. Floor line heavy.
- Doors and windows at their width and height (head height, sill height).
- Fixtures and cabinets: vanity, tub/shower, toilet (side profile only if on that wall), base and upper cabinets, mirrors, medicine cabinets, niches, grab bars, shelving.
- Finish extents: tile areas shaded and labeled with height ("tile to 7'-0""), wainscot, backsplash, accent walls.
- Electrical/plumbing devices only when dictated or clearly visible and relevant to scope (outlets, switches, lights, valves, shower heads).

**Dimensions:** horizontal dimension string along the bottom, vertical dimension string on the side. Every height is labeled with its source (see section 11).

**Decks:** an elevation is drawn from the yard side only when it adds info - height above grade, railing height, stairs and risers, post locations if dictated.

**Sunrooms/porches:** elevations of window walls and knee walls show knee wall height, window sizes and heights, and screen/glass panels.

## 11. Height sources and labels
Heights come from a mix of dictation and photo estimates. Every height on an elevation carries its source:

| Source | Label | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Dictated | no tag | 3'-0" |
| Estimated from a photo | (est.) | 3'-0" (est.) |
| Default, nothing to go on | (assumed) | 6'-8" (assumed) |

**How photo estimates are made:**
- Use the straight-on wall photo. Angled shots and wide-angle distortion make estimates unreliable - if no usable photo exists, fall back to default and mark "(assumed)".
- Scale from a **dictated** dimension in the same photo first (ceiling height, a dictated window or vanity width). Measured references beat standard ones.
- Only when nothing dictated is in frame, scale from common references and note which was used: door height, outlet/switch cover plate, standard tile size if tiles can be counted.
- Round estimates to the nearest inch. If the estimate drives price (tile height, window size) flag it on the missing-info list for confirmation.

**Defaults (only when nothing dictated and no usable photo):**
| Item | Default height |
|---|---|
| Interior door head | 6'-8" |
| Kitchen countertop | 36" |
| Vanity top | 34" |
| Bottom of upper cabinets | 54" AFF |
| Tub rim | 15" (standard alcove tub) |
| Window sill | not defaulted - estimate from photo or flag |

## 12. Labels and units
- **Feet and inches only** on drawings (e.g., 10'-2") - no decimal feet. Quantity math can be done separately in the scope write-up.
- Room/area names, overall dimensions, ceiling height per interior room.
- Any number or construction type not measured or confirmed is marked "(assumed)"; photo-derived heights are marked "(est.)".
- Output is for planning and quoting - not permit or CAD drawings.
